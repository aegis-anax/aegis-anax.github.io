Aegis Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Aegis is a personal AI assistant used by a single person, its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's account is connected to it. This policy explains what Aegis accesses, why, and how that data is handled.

What Aegis accesses

  • Gmail (owner's account): reads recent email to build a daily briefing and to reply in the right thread; creates drafts; sends an email only after the owner reviews the draft and explicitly taps Send.
  • Gmail (Aegis's own account, aegis.anax@gmail.com): reads its own inbox, creates drafts and sends email only after the owner approves each one.
  • Google Calendar: reads the owner's calendars to plan the day, and manages only calendars that Aegis itself created.
  • YouTube Data API: searches public videos for recommendations. No account data is involved.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide Aegis's features to its owner: briefings, planning, reminders and email drafts. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to build profiles for anyone else.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and working data are stored on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the Aegis service. Aegis keeps a short log of sent emails (time, recipients, subject; not the message body).

Who processes it

  • Google provides Gmail, Calendar and YouTube.
  • Anthropic (Claude) processes text needed to answer the owner's requests, such as an email being summarized or drafted, under Anthropic's commercial API terms.
  • General-knowledge questions that contain no personal information may be answered by other AI providers. Email and calendar content is never sent to them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Aegis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke Aegis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all reading and sending immediately; stored tokens are then useless and are deleted.
