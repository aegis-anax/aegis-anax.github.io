Aegis

A private, personal AI assistant. It plans, reminds and drafts, and its user stays in control of every decision.

Plans around real life

Builds each day around school, work and commitments, protects free time, and adapts when plans change.

Morning briefing

Schedule, weather, deadlines and the emails that actually need attention, in one message.

Email drafts

Writes replies and new emails. Nothing is sent until the user reviews it and taps Send.

Goals and deadlines

Tracks goals, far-off deadlines and the people who matter, and nudges at the right time.

Private by design

Aegis is a personal tool for a single user. It runs on its owner's own computer, keeps data there, and is not offered to the public. Read the privacy policy.
